Key Macro-Market Finding

Across the European Union, over one-in-five citizens (22.1% raw, 23.4% weighted) actively participate in digital sharing platforms. Notably, 19.6% of all active participants act as dual prosumers—simultaneously consuming peer services and providing their own assets or labor. Adoption is heavily skewed toward younger cohorts (37.2% among 15-24 year olds vs. 11.2% among 65+), demonstrating strong generational digital affinity.

Prosumer Engagement Index (PEI) Formulation & Interpretation

The PEI is an empirically constructed, formatively specified index (0 to 100) combining four normalized behavioral dimensions:

  • Consumer Frequency (25%): Recoded from d8 (0=Never to 3=Regularly).
  • Provider Frequency (25%): Recoded from d9 (0=Never to 3=Regularly).
  • Sector Activity Breadth (25%): Total collaborative sectors used and offered across Transport, Accommodation, Food, Household, Professional, and Finance (0 to 12).
  • Market Substitution (25%): Depth of replacement of traditional commercial channels (0=None, 1=Partial, 2=Complete).
Empirical Evidence: Dual Prosumers vs. Consumer-Only Users

Dual prosumers demonstrate statistically superior engagement across every behavioral metric compared to single-role consumers: they use 1.9 sectors on average vs. 1.2 sectors (Mann-Whitney U, p < 0.0001), have a 41.3% market substitution rate vs. 29.8%, and exhibit an 82.4% positive recommendation rate vs. 67.1%. Prosumers are the primary catalysts of platform ecosystem growth.

Occasional Frugal Consumers
38.4% of Users • Mean PEI: 11.2

Price-sensitive consumers who use platforms occasionally for single-transaction discounts (e.g. cheap accommodation or rides). Zero provider activity.

Strategy: Low friction onboarding, transparent fees, discovery promos.
Convenience Urbanites
32.8% of Users • Mean PEI: 22.6

Highly educated city dwellers using transport and food delivery multiple times monthly. Value digital ease, app ratings, and time-saving convenience.

Strategy: Subscription passes, app UI optimization, loyalty tiers.
Sustainability Co-Creators
14.6% of Users • Mean PEI: 39.1

Dual-role prosumers motivated by efficient asset use, circular economy sharing, and community interaction. High in repair, household, and peer exchange.

Strategy: Carbon/waste impact badges, community governance, green storytelling.
Entrepreneurial Micro-Providers
14.2% of Users • Mean PEI: 46.8

Active service providers earning primary or supplementary income. Deep multi-sector presence, high platform advocacy, and entrepreneurial focus.

Strategy: Automated tax integration, provider portal, faster payouts.
The Role of Sustainability in Collaborative Value Creation

Over 28.4% of European collaborative service providers explicitly cite 'sustainable and efficient use of available assets' as their motivation for offering services. While economic earnings (supplementary income at 56.2%) remain the primary adoption driver, sustainability-motivated providers operate across significantly more sectors (p < 0.01) and exhibit higher asset recirculating behavior.

Cross-Country Disparities across EU-28

Western and Northern European economies (France: 36.4%, Ireland: 35.1%, Croatia: 34.2%, Estonia: 32.8%) demonstrate mature collaborative ecosystems with widespread prosumption, whereas Southern and Eastern nations (Cyprus: 6.8%, Bulgaria: 11.2%, Greece: 13.5%) face institutional barriers regarding tax compliance and platform distrust.

Executive Marketing & Platform Governance Recommendations

1. Accelerate the Prosumer Transition Funnel

Over 19% of non-providing consumers state they would consider offering services if legal and tax rules were simplified. Platforms should integrate automated 1-click tax reporting and micro-insurance to lower provider onboarding friction.

2. Monetize Asset Idle Capacity

Target under-utilized consumer capital (idle vehicles, spare tools, vacant storage) with targeted supply-side campaigns highlighting monthly supplementary earnings.

3. Institutionalize Circular & Green Storytelling

Quantify environmental savings in consumer dashboards (e.g. kg CO2 avoided by car sharing, items diverted from landfill through peer repair) to engage Persona 3 (Sustainability Co-Creators).

4. Leverage Prosumer Brand Advocacy

Because high-PEI prosumers recommend platforms at an 82.4% rate, platforms should design exclusive prosumer ambassador programs, giving them referral equity and platform voice.
